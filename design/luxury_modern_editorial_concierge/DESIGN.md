## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimacy and elegance of a private boutique hotel combined with an elite bespoke travel concierge. Rejecting tourist clichés—such as faux papyrus, cartoon hieroglyphs, or garish gilded accents—the identity communicates modern Egyptian luxury through confident editorial minimalism, tactile warmth, and spatial poise.

The visual tone is calm, culturally grounded, and discerning. It treats Cairo not as an ancient relic, but as an energetic, sophisticated metropolis where monumental heritage lives alongside mid-century design, contemporary art, and transcendent culinary craft. 

The aesthetic style merges **Minimalism** with subtle **Glassmorphic** precision:
- **Atmospheric Whitespace:** Generous margins, architectural structure, and cinematic framing evoke quiet authority.
- **Editorial Typography:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with ultra-legible, geometric sans-serif UI typography establish an authoritative narrative cadence.
- **Translucent Refinement:** Soft-filtered floating controls, hairline neutral borders, and micro-diffused ambient depth mirror natural Saharan light filtered through limestone courtyards.

## Colors

The color palette captures sun-bleached desert limestone, aged bronze craftwork, and deep basalt shadows. 

- **Primary (`#1F1F1F` - Deep Basalt Charcoal):** Used for commanding typography, primary interactive states, structural dividers, and high-emphasis icons. Avoid pure `#000000` to preserve tonal softness against warm surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#B08D57` - Muted Egyptian Bronze):** Applied deliberately to active indicators, curated badges, rating star clusters, key CTA highlights, and micro-borders on premium interactive cards.
- **Tertiary (`#D9C7A3` - Warm Sandstone):** Acts as an intermediary tone for secondary icon outlines, tag backgrounds, interactive hover states, and inactive indicator dots.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F3EC` - Warm Ivory):** The atmospheric base of the experience, replacing clinical cool whites with the warmth of washed alabaster and raw linen.
- **Surfaces & Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated content containers to create clean layer separation against the ivory canvas.
- **Structural Borders (`#EDE8DF` and `#EADEC9`):** Delicate hairline borders that establish structural definition without heavy visual contrast.

## Typography

The type scale orchestrates a tension between classical editorial grandeur and crisp, contemporary operational clarity:

- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Editorial Titles):** Brings statuesque proportions, delicate bracketed serifs, and high stroke contrast. Used for hero storytelling, destination headers, and section narratives. Keep weights at regular (`400`) or medium (`500`) to retain bookish restraint.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Functional UI, Labels):** Offers clean geometric balance, open counters, and high legibility across small viewport matrices. Ensures data points, itineraries, concierge steps, and form controls read without visual friction.
- **Tracking & Proportion:** Large serif headlines use slight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to bind titles tightly; eyebrow metadata and category labels (`label-caps`) use wide positive letter-spacing (`0.12em`) combined with uppercase transformation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs a fluid 12-column grid system bounded by maximum reading viewports of 1440px to preserve intentional white balance:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` gutters and `5rem` page margins. Editorial sections frequently alternate between full-span visuals, asymmetric 7:5 storytelling pairings, and clean 3- or 4-column destination matrices.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` canvas margins. Asymmetric layouts collapse to uniform 4:4 splits or structured vertical stacks.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` edge margins. Destination cards and curated itinerary components shift into horizontal, peek-ahead carousels.

Spacing rhythm follows a strict 8pt core grid, using larger vertical multipliers (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) between thematic editorial chapters to preserve an unhurried, luxury tempo.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses flat surfaces framed by ambient, desert-tinted shadows, and glassmorphic overlays:

- **Tonal Layers:** Content starts on the `#F7F3EC` Warm Ivory canvas. Active surfaces sit on elevated `#FFFFFF` crisp white containers, framed with a 1px border colored `#EDE8DF` (or `#EADEC9` when elevated).
- **Floating Controls & Glassmorphism:** Primary top navigation bars and pinned itinerary filter bars float across viewport layers using `rgba(247, 243, 236, 0.8)` with a 16px backdrop blur, bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(176, 141, 87, 0.25)`.
- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** Avoid muddy cold drop shadows. All elevation levels utilize warm bronze-charcoal tinting:
  - *Resting Cards:* `0 4px 20px -4px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(176, 141, 87, 0.03)`
  - *Floating Search Bar / Interactive Elevate:* `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(176, 141, 87, 0.05)`
  - *Hover Elevation:* Cards elevate softly on the Y-axis by `-3px` while shifting shadow to `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.1)`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Structural cards and section splits consistently employ a 1px solid `#EDE8DF` boundary, ensuring layout legibility without visual weight.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern geometric curvature. Surfaces, cards, and interactive elements feature refined corners (`roundedness: 2`, matching 16px - 20px for major card groups), balancing architectural precision with welcoming softness.

- **Primary Cards & Modals:** Standardized with `1rem` (16px) or `1.25rem` (20px) radius for destination media containers, itineraries, and modal sheets.
- **Pills & Interactive Badges:** Floating search inputs, concierge duration badges, filter chips, and primary action buttons utilize full-pill radii (`9999px`) to emphasize touch-friendliness and dynamic fluidity against the structured serif typography.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** Employ `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` (8px - 12px) corners for crisp, tactile containment.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action Button:** Full-pill geometry (`rounded-full`), `#1F1F1F` background, `#F7F3EC` text, subtle 1px border in `#1F1F1F`. On hover: transforms to `#B08D57` background with 200ms ease-out transitions.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 1px `#1F1F1F` or `#B08D57` outline, Deep Basalt text. Hover fills background with `rgba(217, 199, 163, 0.15)`.
- **Concierge Quick-Action Pills:** Pill-shaped, crisp `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#EDE8DF` border, paired with an icon colored `#B08D57`.

### Floating Glassmorphic Pill Navigation
- Centered, floating pill docked 24px below top viewport.
- Surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` with 16px backdrop blur, encased in a 1px `rgba(234, 222, 201, 0.7)` hairline stroke.
- Features micro logo mark, serif section links with bronze underline indicators for active views, and a compact "Plan Your Journey" pill button.

### Floating Interactive Travel Search & Concierge Planner Bar
- Horizontal floating capsule card spanning up to 960px, resting at the hero threshold.
- Split into four tactile segments: "Destination / Neighborhood", "Experience Type", "Dates", and "Guests / Style".
- Dividers use 1px vertical hairlines in `#EDE8DF`.
- Includes an integrated search icon button in solid `#1F1F1F` with a soft bronze hover transition.

### Editorial Destination Cards
- Aspect ratio: 4:5 (portrait editorial) or 16:9 (panoramic villas & historic landmarks).
- Container: 16px to 20px border radius, overflow hidden, framed with 1px `#EDE8DF`.
- Media interaction: Image performs a smooth micro-scale zoom (`transform: scale(1.04)`) across 600ms bezier easing on hover.
- Metadata overlay: Clean gradient protection (`rgba(31, 31, 31, 0) 40%` to `rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.75) 100%`) with white Playfair Display titles, Warm Sand duration pills, and Muted Bronze location coordinates.

### Curated Itineraries & Duration Pills
- Cards set on pure `#FFFFFF` background with subtle padding (`space-lg`).
- Includes duration badges featuring an `#F7F3EC` fill, `#B08D57` text, and `0.5rem` rounded corners.
- Chronological timeline indicators connect via 1.5px dashed paths styled in `#D9C7A3`.

### 4-Step Concierge Guide
- Horizontal or 4-column structured layout mapping the concierge intake lifecycle (Curate, Refine, Secure, Experience).
- Large numeral indicators (01, 02, 03, 04) rendered in `Playfair Display` with `#D9C7A3` at 40% opacity.
- Minimalist text copy paired with delicate bronze iconography.

### Form Inputs, Checkboxes & Radios
- **Inputs:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#EDE8DF` border; transitions to 1.5px `#B08D57` outline upon focus with no aggressive glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Deep Charcoal `#1F1F1F` checkmark active fills with subtle Bronze border styling.

### Minimalist Trust Metrics
- Compact stat modules featuring prominent Playfair numerals (e.g., "100%", "24/7", "1:1") over descriptive `label-caps` body copy in `#1F1F1F`.
- Separated by vertical sandstone hairpins without heavy box encasements.
